# P0-H HBUT CAS policy and verification route acceptance

**Decision:** `PARTIAL_PASS_OFFICIAL_HBUT_UPGRADED_CAS_BASIS_AND_REVIEW_OFFICE_VERIFIED_EDITION_AND_JOURNAL_RECORD_PENDING`

**CAS Q3 STATUS: NOT READY.**

On 2026-09-14, the project lead inspected two public pages issued by Hubei
University of Technology's Science and Technology Development Institute. The
[2025 research-workload notice](https://kjcy.hbut.edu.cn/info/1054/8295.htm)
states that SCI and SSCI partitioning uses the upgraded CAS basis, requires
Hubei University of Technology as first affiliation for reported papers, and
routes research outputs through college review. It also lists a contact route
for natural-science papers. The institute's [office-responsibility
page](https://kjcy.hbut.edu.cn/bflm/jgsz/nsjg.htm) assigns natural-science paper
statistics, analysis and recognition review to the Natural Science Vertical
Projects and Achievements Management Office.

This closes two narrower uncertainties: the university publicly uses the CAS
upgraded basis, and the relevant institutional review route is identifiable.
It does **not** identify the institution-recognized edition/year, retain a
current Applied Intelligence title/ISSN record showing Computer Science
major-category Q3 or above, or establish the university's title/ISSN-change
treatment. The notice links two policy attachments, but their download endpoint
requires a CAPTCHA; their contents were not inspected or inferred.

An official [School of Science graduate training
plan](https://lxy.hbut.edu.cn/info/1112/3890.htm) provides secondary evidence:
it says the upgraded CAS report from the year before publication controls its
research-paper partitioning. For a paper formally published in 2026, this gives
2025 as the candidate report year. Because this is a college programme rule
rather than a central research-administration decision, it does not prove that
the same rule governs university-wide faculty-output recognition. The
[bounded secondary-evidence record](P0_H_HBUT_CAS_EDITION_RULE_SECONDARY_EVIDENCE.md)
preserves that distinction.

P0-H therefore remains open. The responsible author should request and retain
one record from the named office that states the applicable edition/year,
Applied Intelligence's current title and ISSNs, its Computer Science major
category tier, and the recognition rule for any title or ISSN change. This
record does not authorize submission.
