## One decision rule

1. Query the institution-recognized CAS edition using the current title and
   current ISSNs for *Discover Computing* and *Journal of Intelligent
   Information Systems*.
2. If *Discover Computing* is Tier 3 under the exact recognized category and the
   authors accept its open-access funding terms, select it. Its current scope
   explicitly includes artificial intelligence, machine learning and natural
   language processing, and its review policy emphasizes accurate reporting and
   fundamental validity rather than significance.
3. Otherwise, if *Journal of Intelligent Information Systems* is Tier 3, select
   it and accept the higher desk risk from its stronger originality/advance
   expectations.
4. Use *Journal of Information Science* only after both CAS qualification and
   an editorial-scope check. Retain current *Natural Language Processing* as a
   last fallback because its novel-method preference conflicts with this
   empirical-only paper and its recent title change creates a CAS identity risk.
5. If none both qualifies and accepts the current bounded Claim, stop or change
   journal. Do not add experiments or enlarge Claims merely to force fit.
