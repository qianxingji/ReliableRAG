The official Discover Computing page was checked on 2026-09-14. The journal is
fully open access and applies an APC if an article is accepted. Its current
listed alternatives are GBP 1,040, USD 1,520, or EUR 1,140, with VAT or local
taxes where applicable. The governing price is the price on the article's
acceptance date, so the current values are evidence for owner planning rather
than a future-price guarantee.

Springer Nature states that institutional open-access agreements may cover the
charge and that institutional or funder support may be available. Waivers or
discounts exist for corresponding authors in the lowest-income countries.
Other requests may be considered case by case for financial need. A
discretionary waiver or discount must be requested at initial manuscript
submission; the existence of that route is not evidence that a waiver has been
granted.

Official source: <https://link.springer.com/journal/10791/how-to-publish-with-us>
