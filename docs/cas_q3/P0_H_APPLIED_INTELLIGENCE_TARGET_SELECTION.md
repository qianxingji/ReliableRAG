## Scientific fit and rejection risk

The paper's controlled evaluation of post-generation RAG repair selection is
within the journal's broad intelligent-systems scope. The fit is conditional,
because the journal describes itself as a platform for new and original
research and technological developments, while the accepted study deliberately
claims no new selector architecture. Its strongest result is a bounded,
comparison-dependent empirical attribution on one Qwen reader: HGB+GbV passes
against the supervision-matched GbV-only head, does not pass against HGB-only,
and ROA-FULL does not advance.

The main desk/reviewer risks therefore remain:

1. the contribution may be judged too narrow or insufficiently original;
2. one accepted 3B reader does not establish model-family or scale transfer;
3. the positive comparison is not a joint advantage over HGB-only;
4. original fit-time estimator receipts and an independent original-fit witness
   remain missing; and
5. standalone deployment latency, end-to-end cost and complete peak-memory
   measurements were not captured.

Changing the target journal does not authorize new experiments or broader
Claims. The empirical/negative-result framing and all failed Phi, stopped
Mistral and rejected ROA results remain part of the record.

- documentation of substantive LLM use in the Methods or another suitable
