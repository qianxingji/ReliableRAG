Decision: **PASS_BOUNDED_HBUT_PUBLIC_TARGET_RECORD_RECHECK_NO_QUALIFYING_2025_INSTITUTIONAL_RECORD_FOUND.**

**CAS Q3 STATUS: NOT READY.**

On 2026-09-15 the project repeated a domain-restricted public search for an
HBUT record that simultaneously binds the 2025 CAS upgraded edition, Computer
Science major category, Applied Intelligence's current title and both ISSNs,
and a Q3-or-better tier. No qualifying public record was found within the
bounded search.

Four candidate pages were inspected. An official School of Computer Science
and Artificial Intelligence faculty page labels one 2022 Applied Intelligence
article “SCI 2区.” An official Big Data and Artificial Intelligence school page
uses the same label for 2022 and 2023 articles. Neither page identifies the CAS
versus JCR basis, the edition, the major category, or the current title/ISSN
rule. An official Graduate School page lists a 2026 Applied Intelligence article
without a partition label. A 2014 HBUT faculty-assessment document distinguishes
SCI 1/2 from SCI 3 and later, but predates the CAS upgraded-edition requirement
and does not identify Applied Intelligence.

These pages cannot replace the retained institutional record required by P0-H.
The search does not prove that no record exists: search-engine coverage is not
complete, authenticated or internal pages were not searched, and the
captcha-protected attachments already disclosed in the HBUT policy audit remain
uninspected. The earlier official evidence that HBUT uses the CAS upgraded basis
is not invalidated. P0-H still requires an HBUT-recognized record for the 2025
edition, current journal identity, major-category tier and title/ISSN-change
treatment.

The first top-level gate run after adding this record failed because the
evidence index still pinned the reviewer-verification receipt produced before
the map gained these two files. That sequencing failure is retained here; the
resolution is to pin the newly generated reviewer receipt and rerun the
fail-closed gate. It is not a scientific-result or CAS-classification failure.

This audit read no scientific payload, executed no model forward and performed
no fit. It does not authorize submission.
