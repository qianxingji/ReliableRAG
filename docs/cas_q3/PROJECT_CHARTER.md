# ReliableRAG CAS Q3 project charter

Effective 2026-09-13 from the user's explicit target transition.

## Goal and authority

Deliver a truthful, complete, reproducible paper that is submission-ready for a
CAS Journal Ranking Q3 venue. Verify the target journal's partition year, ISSN,
major/minor category and institutional recognition rule before declaring final
readiness. Until those facts and every evidence gate are closed, report
**`CAS Q3 STATUS: NOT READY`**.

Work remains the client Research Project Lead. Routine engineering, replay and
ordinary analysis use Sol High; major method/claim decisions and final
authenticity, fairness, reviewer and Submission Ready audits use GPT-6 Astra
xhigh, following the existing routing policy in `../cas_q2/MODEL_ROUTING.md`.

The repository remains for experimental research code and audit documentation.
Do not write the manuscript until the user explicitly authorizes manuscript
work. Research design and claim boundaries precede implementation.

## Scientific requirements

- Preserve every negative result, failed namespace, seed and original artifact.
- Never tune on test labels, select favorable subgroups or change a frozen
  threshold after seeing results.
- Match supervision, candidate pool, information availability, eligibility,
  action budget and tie rules for every claimed comparison.
- Report full-population EM, Rescue, Damage, Neutral, action count and complete
  acquisition/scoring cost boundaries.
- Distinguish accepted Qwen evidence from failed Phi evidence and unexecuted
  Mistral plans.
- Keep HGB as an upstream signal/comparator and HGB_GBV_R as an empirical policy;
  neither is a newly established top-level algorithm.
- Match each claim to its exact reader, datasets, retrievers, model revision,
  frozen policy and evaluation population.
- Do not use engineering rigor, trace volume or a third reader as a substitute
  for scientific contribution.

## Current scientific object

The only currently supportable paper direction is a bounded empirical audit of
fixed paired RAG repair under supervision-matched controls, full-population harm
and a 5% batch action budget. Qwen results establish a joint advantage for
HGB_GBV_R over GBV_ONLY_R, do not establish a joint advantage over HGB_ONLY_R,
and do not clear ROA-FULL. A Q3 paper may present this comparison-dependent
boundary if the contribution, precision, cost, provenance, release and journal
scope can withstand final review.

The independent GPT-6 Astra xhigh
[target-transition audit](TARGET_TRANSITION_ASTRA_XHIGH_AUDIT.md) sets the unique
route to
`PROCEED_Q3_QWEN_ONLY_EVIDENCE_AND_JOURNAL_FIT_GATE_NO_NEW_EXPERIMENTS`.
It accepts the existing Qwen study as a possible scientific core, not as a
Submission Ready decision or journal-acceptance guarantee.

Phi and Mistral are not prerequisites unless a later prospective governance
decision explicitly changes the scope. The current terminal decisions prohibit
their execution or use as scientific evidence.

## Acceptance rule

Submission Ready requires all of the following:

1. a specific empirical research question and contribution distinct from close
   prior work without claiming a new algorithm;
2. complete, fair presentation of the strongest controls and all negative
   findings, including HGB_ONLY_R and rejected ROA results;
3. exact traceability from reported numbers to sealed Qwen evidence, protocols,
   commits, configs, seeds and environments;
4. a reproducibility and release package whose declared scope and limitations are
   independently verified;
5. fixed title, abstract-level claims, tables/figures plan and limitation text
   that do not overstate reader or population generality;
6. a target journal verified as CAS Q3 under the user's applicable rule and a
   documented fit to its scope;
7. an Astra xhigh final fairness, claim, simulated-reviewer and Submission Ready
   audit with no unresolved P0 issue.

P0 blocks credible submission, P1 materially improves competitiveness, and P2
is optional. Lowering the target tier never changes this evidence hierarchy.
