automatically relicensed by the project-code license unless a file or release
manifest expressly says so.

The reviewer aggregate candidate excludes dataset questions, contexts and
answers; Qwen, BGE and DeBERTa weights/tokenizers; dependency wheels; generated
answers; per-question outcomes; learned estimators; and private reproduction
archives. Its detailed inventory is in
`release/cas_q3_aggregate_v2/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
